Night Shift, made yours.

Nightcap brings Night Shift to your menu bar. Set how warm it gets, switch it or peek past it with a shortcut, and keep it off while the apps and websites you choose are in front.

Download for Mac macOS 15 or later, Apple silicon
How it works

It follows what's in front

Pick the apps, and the websites in Safari or Chrome, that need true colour. While one is in front, Night Shift stays off. Switch away and it comes back, and your schedule carries on as before. Rules work the other way too: keep Night Shift on for the app you read in at night, even with it off everywhere else.

One shortcut, two moves

Set a shortcut in Settings, ⌃⌥⌘N to start with, and it works from any app. A tap switches Night Shift. A hold is a peek: true colour until you let go, then the warmth comes straight back.

Try the shortcut: tap the keys, then hold them.

Overrule it anytime

Snooze a rule from its app's row in the menu, for half an hour or until morning. Or turn Night Shift on in Control Center while a rule holds it off: Nightcap goes along with you, and snoozes that rule until the schedule's next change instead of fighting you.

Made to be scripted

Everything in the menu is also a nightcap command. Bind it to a key with skhd, Raycast or Karabiner, run it from a script, or read the status as JSON.

Rules, and Night Shift's strength and schedule, can also live in a plain config file, next to the rest of your setup.

$ nightcap off --for 1h30m
Night Shift off until 01:00
$ nightcap rules add domain:figma.com
Added domain:figma.com, which turns Night Shift off. Night Shift off until 01:00.
$ nightcap rules add app:com.apple.iBooksX --on
Added app:com.apple.iBooksX, which turns Night Shift on. Night Shift off until 01:00.
$ nightcap reset
Night Shift following the schedule
$ nightcap status --json | jq -r .decision.state
on
