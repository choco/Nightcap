The three menu-bar moons, exactly as the app ships them from `app/Nightcap/Assets.xcassets`: `menubar-on.svg` (filled: Night Shift on), `menubar-off.svg` (outline: off) and `menubar-forced-off.svg` (outline with a "z": forced off). They are macOS template images, single-ink in black (`#000000`) on a 24 grid with 2px strokes: on a dark ground, draw them inline in `ink` instead, as the `Menu` component does.
